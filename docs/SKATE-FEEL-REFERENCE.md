# Skate feel reference

**Partial static reference, 22 October 2026:** an existing private export contains modern EA **skate.** push, steering, friction, jump and pump settings. These are stored asset values, not verified active gameplay values. Gravity, braking, trick/grab timing, grind capture, bail thresholds and camera settings remain unresolved. Native behavior and feel acceptance remain unverified. Only selected numbers and original descriptions are included; game files, code, assets, footage, credentials and raw output stay outside Git.

Mod baseline: [main `34f6201`](https://github.com/Dj-Shortcut/mw2-rust-skate-rewrite/commit/34f6201d09a266c87c8c86a935d9fe72af911aef). A comparison with main `9e5bd9d` on 22 October found no changes in the client/shared files cited below. Later source changes require a new comparison. **Code** = declaration plus actual client call site read; **derived** = arithmetic, not measurement. Seconds follow clocks/dt, degrees follow angles, metres/m/s/m/s² assume the project’s Unity-world convention (scale unmeasured). Mutable initializers are defaults; installed configuration, server caps and native effects remain unknown.

## Reference provenance and confidence

**R** below means asset `gameplay/skatephysicstuning`, root `SkatePhysicsTuningAsset`, found through `Data/Win32/levels/game/bam_levelroot/bam_levelroot.toc` and its referenced bundle/CAS payload. Field paths and record numbers identify selected entries in the private `typed-r.out` report. The data was read on 19 October and received on the Mac on 20 October; on 22 October its SHA-256 was independently checked against the transfer manifest: `7b233123989bcf4fdd97e39a4d16a2309c89ce7955b2d67357572e4bc9a7f04d`. Descriptor output and its receipt also match their manifest hashes. Capture metadata records Steam App **3,354,750**, Build **25,617,022**. The same build number was seen on Shadow on 22 October; the current installation's content hash was not rechecked. Individual source CAS names/payload hashes are absent from this export.

The private offline reader used ReSkate revision [`fffd34c`](https://github.com/Dingo-Shenanigans/ReSkate/tree/fffd34ca8035582fa6307e8104c6a7cab350db18), decoded the asset's actual serialized descriptors, and reported no truncation/unexpanded values, with **two unresolved external references**. It emits only keyword-selected paths: a missing row does not establish that the asset or game lacks the setting. This does not validate ReSkate's older runtime profile for this game build. No new decoder or game process was run for this comparison.

**S** = high confidence that the selected stored number is faithfully transcribed, rounded here for readability; active mode, consumer and physical units remain unverified. Length/speed/acceleration fields use **game units (GU), GU/s or GU/s² provisionally**, inferred from names, with **low unit confidence**; GU = metre has not been established. Scalar/coefficient interpretation is provisional too. `LookaheadTime_S` and `*AngleDeg` explicitly name seconds/degrees (**medium unit confidence**); active use is still unknown. Curve pairs below are stored **raw x/y coordinates**, not a reconstructed evaluator or measurement. Inline samples and referenced FloatCurve points can differ; neither is selected as authoritative here. **N** = not recovered in this bounded export, not a game-wide absence finding.

## Proposed changes to our formulas; owner approval required

These are independent, reversible **test candidates**, not recovered EA formulas or a claim of matched feel. No runtime code/configuration has changed.

1. **Push:** try a **9.5 m/s** push ceiling and a **1.5 m/s total contact impulse**, removing the separate 2.8 m/s² continuous drive while a stroke runs. Normalize the contact impulse over the actual planted phase and retain downhill momentum/fakie sign. Expected effect: less motor-like acceleration and a clearer foot-driven surge. The 9.25/1.5 stored fields motivate the experiment; their physical units and exact role remain unverified. Measure complete acceleration curves before accepting it.
2. **Carve:** first retain our 150°/s speed law and 7/10 s⁻¹ response, but try **sign(input) × abs(input)^1.6** after existing input calibration. At half input and 8 m/s, steady target rate becomes **24.74°/s** instead of 37.5°/s; full input stays 75°/s. Expected effect: calmer small corrections without losing the full turn. This exponent is our candidate, not EA's curve. Fit speed/radius next using angular speed = speed/radius in radians/s, then convert to degrees/s; fit lag separately.
3. **Pop:** at our existing gravity **16 m/s²**, independently try a movement-body height band **1.35–1.575 m** with launch speed **sqrt(2 × gravity × height) = 6.573–7.099 m/s**. Ideal flat-ground airtime becomes **0.822–0.887 s**, compared with the current algebraic 0.66–0.75 s. Expected effect: more height and trick time; it could also feel too floaty. This assumes the unverified GU-to-metre interpretation and is not an EA airtime measurement. Keep board/feet animation lift separate. A measured pair h/T would instead determine gravity = 8h/T² and pop speed = 4h/T under equal-height ballistic assumptions; do not tune gravity from airtime alone.
4. **Pump:** prototype our existing **12 J/kg per-pass cap** spread over a **0.2 s** supported transition window, with an original gain taper from full at **12 m/s** to zero at **16 m/s** and no gain on flat terrain. Preserve travel sign and contact loss behavior. Expected effect: a smoother bottom acceleration and less repeated high-speed gain. These timing/speed limits are authored candidates, not translations of EA's 13 or its curves. Compare entry/exit energy on the same transition; COM/curvature coupling remains a separate design gap.
5. **Coasting, brake, grind, bail and camera:** retain current numbers until the corresponding units or measurements are available. Distinguish material/contact coefficients from deceleration. For camera, measure distance/follow/FOV independently from actual speed before proposing replacement numbers; the export supports none.
